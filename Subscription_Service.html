BirdKam Pro Automatic Subscription Service

1. Subscription Name

The subscription service is called BirdKam Pro.

2. Service Options

BirdKam Pro offers two types of subscription services:

  • BirdKam Pro Monthly
  • BirdKam Pro Yearly

3. Subscription Price

The subscription prices are as follows:

  • Monthly subscription: $10.99/month
  • Yearly subscription: $109.99/year

The currency and price of the subscription will be automatically converted from the US dollar price to the currency of the country or region where your Google Account is located based on your region.

4. Automatic Renewal

Your Google account will be automatically charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of each billing cycle. The membership duration will be extended for the corresponding subscription service (monthly or yearly).

5. Canceling Subscription

If you wish to cancel your BirdKam Pro subscription, follow these steps:

  • Open Settings on your device.
  • Go to Google Account.
  • Enter the Account Settings page.
  • Select Subscribe and then choose the BirdKam Pro subscription to cancel.

Please note, the subscription will automatically renew if not canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current subscription period.
