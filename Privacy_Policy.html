Privacy Policy - BirdKam Snap

Welcome to the privacy policy of BirdKam Snap. This document outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you interact with our mobile app, which works with BirdKam cameras for bird watching enthusiasts. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it by complying with relevant data protection laws.

1. Information We Collect

We collect various types of information to provide and improve our services. This includes:

  • Personal Information: This includes your name, email address, and other identifying information you provide when registering an account or making in-app purchases.
  • Camera Data: Data related to your BirdKam camera, such as camera status, configuration settings, and the images and videos captured by the camera, which are uploaded to our servers for storage and processing.
  • Device Information: Information about the mobile device you use to interact with BirdKam Snap, including device model, operating system version, IP address, and other technical identifiers.
  • Location Data: With your permission, we may collect approximate geolocation data from your device to enhance camera setup and provide location-based features, such as determining the best environment for bird watching.
  • Usage Data: We collect information on how you interact with BirdKam Snap, including which features you use, frequency of use, and in-app activities. This helps us improve the app and offer personalized experiences.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for various purposes, including:

  • App Functionality: To enable the primary features of BirdKam Snap, including querying the status of your BirdKam camera, setting up the camera, and receiving image and video transmissions.
  • AI Recognition: We use AI technology to identify birds in the images and videos captured by your BirdKam camera. This helps provide valuable insights for bird watchers.
  • Personalization: To provide a more tailored experience based on your preferences, usage patterns, and location data (if shared).
  • Subscriptions and Payments: To manage your subscription to premium features, including processing in-app purchases and renewals securely through our payment processors.
  • Customer Support: To assist with technical issues, respond to inquiries, and provide customer support.
  • Security and Fraud Prevention: To maintain the security and integrity of our services, protect your data from unauthorized access, and detect or prevent fraud.
  • App Improvement: We analyze usage data to improve BirdKam Snap’s performance, features, and user experience.

3. Data Storage and Retention

We store your data securely on both local devices and our cloud servers. All data uploaded to our servers is encrypted to ensure protection from unauthorized access. Your data is retained as long as you continue to use the BirdKam Snap app and its services. If you choose to delete your account, your data will be erased from our servers within a reasonable time, except where retention is required by law.

4. In-App Payments and Subscription

BirdKam Snap offers subscription-based services that are purchased directly within the app. All payments are processed securely by third-party payment processors. We do not store any payment information on our servers. Your subscription details, including status and renewal dates, are stored securely within the app to manage your access to premium features.

5. Data Sharing and User Consent

We respect your right to control your data. BirdKam Snap enables you to share images and videos captured by your camera within the app. Any sharing of data outside of the app will be under your control, and we do not share your personal data with third parties without your explicit consent, except for service providers that assist us in delivering app functionality (such as cloud storage providers).

6. Data Security Measures

Your privacy is paramount, and we implement advanced security measures to protect your data. This includes encryption of data during transmission and storage, use of secure server environments, and access controls to prevent unauthorized access. We also conduct regular security audits to ensure ongoing protection.

7. AI Recognition and Data Processing

BirdKam Snap uses AI technology to enhance your bird watching experience by identifying bird species from the images and videos captured by your BirdKam camera. This feature processes your data using both local processing on your device and secure cloud-based algorithms. Data collected for AI recognition is anonymized and used to improve the accuracy of our algorithms over time.

8. Your Rights

You have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • Access: You can request access to the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: If your data is inaccurate or incomplete, you can request a correction.
  • Deletion: You can request the deletion of your data under certain conditions, such as if the data is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.
  • Data Portability: You can request a copy of your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw Consent: If we process your data based on consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or relevant legal requirements. When changes are made, we will notify you by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and providing a notice through the BirdKam Snap app or via email. Please review this policy regularly to stay informed about how we protect your data.
